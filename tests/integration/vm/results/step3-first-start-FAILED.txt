## Root cause (found by Andy on swearengen, 2026-11-14)

Not Skep. KVM on the Proxmox host hit an emulation failure on bhyve's
first VMLAUNCH inside hearst: the QEMU journal shows "KVM internal error.
Suberror: 1" with the instruction bytes 0f 01 c2 (VMLAUNCH). This is the
known nested-bhyve-on-KVM bug; the guest VM (hearst) was stopped by KVM,
which is why nothing inside FreeBSD logged or dumped anything. hearst was
stopped and started from Proxmox: reboot 1 of the 2 allowed.

Workaround to apply, pending approval: APIC virtual interrupt delivery off
in vmm (hw.vmm.vmx.cap.virtual_interrupt_delivery=0; the older name
hw.vmm.vmx.use_apic_vid is also read, both in
/usr/src/sys/amd64/vmm/intel/vmx.c of 15.1-RELEASE-p6, lines 874-881).
Before the change: hw.vmm.vmx.cap.virtual_interrupt_delivery: 1,
hw.vmm.vmx.cap.posted_interrupts: 1.
